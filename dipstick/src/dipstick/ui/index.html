Dipstick

Sweeps from 1 MHz up to 200 MHz (how high depends on Longest cable), outside the ham bands. Take the antenna off and leave the cable's far end open or shorted. Give the velocity factor to measure the length, or the length to measure the velocity factor.

Calibrating shows the NanoVNA what its own connector and cable look like, so it can take them out of every measurement. Use the calibration kit that came with it (open, short and 50-ohm load), screwed onto CH0, the port you measure antennas on, with any adapter or jumper you'll measure through. The new calibration covers this band only and isn't saved in the NanoVNA.
